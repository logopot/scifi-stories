# Prompts za slike: „Jedra do Više obale“

Slike su zajedničke za oba jezika. Fajlovi idu u `public/img/jedra-vise-obale/` kao `<id>.jpg` ili `<id>.png`, a zatim `npm run images -- jedra-vise-obale` pravi `<id>.webp`. Slike se ne dodaju u repo bez ovog koraka.

## Zajednički stil (mesta)

Painterly science-fiction illustration, matte gouache and oil texture, a muted desaturated palette of sun-bleached teal, sand, salt white and weathered sailcloth grey with one warm accent of amber ochre, soft diffuse light, subtle film grain, calm and wistful mood. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no real people, no brands, no flags.

## Zajednički stil (portreti)

Painterly bust portrait, matte gouache and oil texture, muted desaturated palette of sun-bleached teal, sand and salt white with a hint of amber ochre, soft diffuse light, subtle film grain, plain dark teal background, face centered in a 3:4 frame and readable when cropped larger, head and shoulders, looking slightly off-camera, no text, no logos, no watermark, no real people.

## Slike mesta (16:9, 1680×944)

### cover.webp

Cover, 16:9 (1680x944). The old cargo sailing ship Dugi Dah, three masts with patched sails of many shades of grey and blue, seen from a three-quarter front angle and sailing on a calm teal sea toward a low green shore far away at dusk. Two pale moons close together above the ship: one at about 36% from the left and 22% from the top, the other at about 60% from the left and 24% from the top. The focal point (the ship) sits in the middle third of the frame. Wide calm water with soft reflections in the lower middle, darker and calm bottom third, no important detail at the left and right edges.

### paluba.webp

Place, 16:9. The deck of Dugi Dah in the soft light between night and day, empty of people: patched sails frame the sky, a barrel and a coil of rope in the lower foreground, wooden planks worn pale by salt. Two pale moons hang in the upper half, one at about 36% from the left and 20% from the top, the other at about 62% from the left and 22% from the top, with a gap of three palm widths between them. Main subject (masts and sails) in the upper half near the center, calm darker bottom third (deck planks and shadow), nothing important at the left and right edges.

### pesak.webp

Place, 16:9. Bright, hazy sun at about 52% from the left and 18% from the top. A wide, clean, calm stretch of water with soft reflections in the lower middle of the frame, village in the upper-middle, calm darker bottom third, no readable signs, nothing important at the edges.

### gvozdje.webp

Place, 16:9. Place, 16:9. The island Burad in morning light: an island built from thousands of barrels bound together with nets, small salt fields on top shining white, a few ropes and a simple pier. ### jarbol.webp

Place, 16:9. A storm at sea seen at the mast of Dugi Dah: a dark teal and slate sky, heavy rain, a patched sail straining, a rope-wrapped mast. Grey waves in the lower middle, deck and dark water in a calm darker bottom third, nothing important at the left and right edges, no people.

### kapija.webp

Place, 16:9. The gate of the Higher Shore at dusk: two narrow white stone pillars with an arch of rope, behind them a low dark green island. Above the gate the two moons overlap into one large pale white disc at about 50% from the left and 14% from the top. A single warm lantern hangs on the left pillar at about 50% from the left and 40% from the top (centered on the gate). A wide, calm water surface in the lower middle with reflections of the lantern and the moons, calm darker bottom third, no people, nothing important at the edges.

### more.webp

Place, 16:9. Calm sea after rain at night, glassy and quiet: the two moons overlapped as one large pale disc at about 50% from the left and 18% from the top, a thin veil of clouds dispersing. A very small rowing boat with a tiny lantern sits far away at about 48% from the left and 62% from the top. A wide clean water surface with long reflections in the lower middle, calm darker bottom third, nothing important at the left and right edges.
