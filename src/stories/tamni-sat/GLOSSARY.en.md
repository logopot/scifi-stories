# Tamni sat → The Dark Hour: glossary (sr → en)

Names of people stay the same (Neda, Bora, Ruža, Dario, Kosta). Invented proper names of places and machines stay the same
(Greben, Lantar, Svod, Automat); ordinary nouns are translated. The choice below is used throughout the story.

## Tokens and form

- `{ime}` stays; `{voc}` becomes `{ime}` (no vocative in English); `{IME}` stays.
- No `tf`, `leadf`, `textf` fields: one neutral text (second person “you”, present tense).
- Speech in curly double quotes “ ”; characters’ names in `who` are unchanged.
