# Pre plime → Before the Tide: glossary (sr → en)

Names of people stay the same (Jela, Anka, Emil, Pavle). The machine name Tok and the company name Plod stay (speaker label / invented name).
Place names that are ordinary words (Plićak, Nasip, Niska strana...) are translated so the English reader understands them;
the choice below is used throughout the story.

## Tokens and form

- `{ime}` stays; `{voc}` becomes `{ime}` (no vocative in English); `{IME}` stays.
- No `tf`, `leadf`, `textf` fields: one neutral text (second person “you”, present tense).
- Speech in curly double quotes “ ”; characters’ names in `who` are unchanged.
