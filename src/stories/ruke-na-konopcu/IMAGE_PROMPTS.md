## Mesta

### cover.webp (16:9)

A cliff-top harbor town seen at blue dusk: a pale limestone cliff with a few stacked houses on the left and center, and a row of tall iron mooring masts reaching out into the air above a vast sea of fog and cloud. Three large patched cargo airships hang in the haze near the masts, each with a small amber lamp. Small amber lamps run along the masts. Focal point in the middle third of the frame: the central mast with the nearest airship. Lilac glow on the horizon, fog below. The bottom third is dark cliff and fog.

### dok.webp (16:9)

The Upper Dock at night in thick fog: a wide iron platform with wet metal plates, three tall mooring masts fading into white fog, each with a lamp. A single large amber lamp on the nearest mast, about 38% from the left and 40% from the top, glowing in the fog. Smaller lamps further away in the haze at the center. No people in the middle of the frame; the lower third is dark wet iron plates and fog. Quiet, tense, cold.

A porcelain cup with a lid on the table. Stone walls, a small door on the right with fog behind it. No people. The bottom third is the dark wooden floor.

### kupola.webp (16:9)

The Dome control room: a round room with a huge curved glass wall showing only white fog outside. A woman's coat hangs over a chair low at the right. No people's heads or shoulders in front of the screens; any figures stay low in the frame below the screens. The bottom third is dark floor and desk.

### uzarnica.webp (16:9)

The Rope Loft: a long low hall with heavy wooden beams, thick ropes hanging from the beams like dried rivers, crates and rope bundles on the floor. A clearly visible iron stove with a painted flame at about 62% from the left and 56% from the top, warm amber glow around it, not hidden behind objects. Silhouettes of a few resting workers sit low in the frame, below the stove height, none in front of the flame. Tar, wool and smoke atmosphere. The bottom third is dark floor.

The Lift Works: a vast hall with three enormous cylindrical gas tanks like towers, pipes and ladders, ribbed metal floor. A row of round pressure gauges glowing pale green side by side at the center, at about 40–58% from the left and 32% from the top. A big iron valve wheel with yellow handles on a wall to the left of center, lower. A faint white gas wisp. The bottom third is dark ribbed floor with a little shadow. No people in the middle of the frame.

### most.webp (16:9)

A narrow iron gantry bridge between two mooring masts, hanging in fog: three paces wide, with a rope instead of a railing, running toward the center. A row of six hanging amber lamps along the bridge at about 30–70% from the left and 37–40% from the top, each smeared into a small glow by the fog. White fog streams upward from below. Faint shapes of masts in the distance. No people in the upper middle. The bottom third is dark iron and fog.

### magla.webp (16:9)

The belly of a huge patched cargo airship emerging from thick fog above an iron dock, seen from below at the center of the frame: stitched panels, seams and patches, thin ropes hanging down. One large amber lamp on the keel at about 50% from the left and 34% from the top. Four smaller lamps along the dock in the fog below it, at 34–66% horizontally. Dense white fog everywhere. No people in the upper middle. The bottom third is dark dock and fog.

### zora.webp (16:9)

The Upper Dock at dawn after the fog has gone: nine airships moored in a calm row to iron masts, pale lilac and rose sky, the low sun about 62% from the left and 30% from the top, soft warm light on wet iron plates, thin mist only near the ground.
