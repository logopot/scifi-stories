## Decisions that were hard

- **Poravnanje** → “the Settlement Board”: the Serbian word means a settlement or reconciliation; “Settlement” keeps the arbitration sense without sounding like a court.
- **uzgon / Pogon uzgona** → “lift” and “the Lift Works”: shorter and more natural than “buoyancy plant”.
- **overiti** → “certify”: keeps the bureaucratic weight; “stamp” appears only for the physical act (“the seal comes down”).
- **svetlosni govor** → “lamp signals”/“lamp talk”: used sparingly, since the Serbian “govor” is the idea of a spoken language.
- **Kupola** → “the Dome”: renamed during Serbian proofreading from a word used in an earlier story.
